Three-Way Matching & AP Reconciliation

A technical reference for accounting mechanics, variance logic, and the end-to-end P2P (Procure-to-Pay) flow.

1. The End-to-End AP Flow (P2P)

The Procure-to-Pay process connects purchasing to accounts payable, ensuring a company only pays for goods it actually ordered and received.

  1. Purchase Order (PO): Procurement creates a document authorizing purchase. Contains item, quantity, agreed unit price, and terms. Status: Commitment, no accounting entry yet.
  2. Goods Receipt Note (GRN): Inventory/Receiving logs the arrival of items. Triggers inventory update and liability accrual.
  3. Invoice Receipt (IR): Vendor submits the bill. Accounts Payable enters it into the ERP.
  4. Reconciliation (Matching): System compares PO, GRN, and Invoice. If they match within tolerance, the invoice is cleared for payment.
  5. Payment: Treasury executes the cash transfer (ACH/Wire/Check), settling the liability.

2. Matching Strategies

Type What is compared Use Case
2-Way Match PO ↔ Invoice Services, intangibles, utilities (where physical receipt isn't tracked). Checks if billed amount matches approved PO.
3-Way Match PO ↔ GRN ↔ Invoice Physical goods. Ensures exactly what was ordered is what was received, and what was received is what is billed.
4-Way Match PO ↔ GRN ↔ Inspection ↔ Invoice High-QA items (e.g., manufacturing components). Only goods that passed quality control (accepted receipt) approve the invoice.

3. The Double-Entry Mechanics: GR/IR Clearing Account

The Goods Receipt / Invoice Receipt (GR/IR) account is a temporary clearing account bridging the timing gap between receiving goods and receiving the invoice. It solves the matching principle problem in accrual accounting.

4. Variances & Mathematics

When the 3 documents do not align perfectly, a variance is generated. Most ERPs block the invoice automatically if variances exceed set Tolerance Limits.

Price Variance (PPV - Purchase Price Variance)

Occurs when the vendor bills a different unit price than agreed on the PO.

  • Expected Amount = Invoice Qty × PO Unit Price
  • Price Variance = Invoice Amount - Expected Amount
Scenario: PO is for 100 widgets @ $10.00/ea.
Vendor ships 100. (GR/IR credited $1,000).
Invoice arrives: 100 widgets @ $10.50/ea (Total: $1,050).

Accounting: The extra $50 is a Price Variance. It must be posted either to an expense account (Purchase Price Variance Account) or capitalized to Inventory, depending on costing methods.
Debit GR/IR $1,000, Debit PPV $50, Credit AP $1,050.

Quantity Variance (Invoice vs Receipt)

Occurs when the vendor bills for a different quantity than was physically received. ERPs typically expect you to pay only for received quantities.

  • Expected Amount = GRN Qty × PO Unit Price
  • Quantity Variance = Invoice Qty - GRN Qty

Tolerance Thresholds

To avoid escalating over negligible differences (like rounding errors or slight raw material weight differences), companies set structural tolerances. An invoice clears automatically if the variance falls within the band.

  • Percentage: e.g., ± 1% of total invoice value.
  • Absolute value limit: e.g., ± $5.00 limit. (Whichever is lower usually wins limit logic).
  • Qty tolerances: e.g., 5% over/under delivery accepted (common in bulk liquids/chemicals).

5. Exception Workflows & Pain Points

Common Exception States

  • Price Block Invoice unit price exceeds PO + Tolerance. Sent to Buyer/Purchasing manager to negotiate with vendor or approve the increase.
  • Qty Block Invoice qty > Received qty. Sent to Warehouse manager to check if goods were left on the dock unprocessed.
  • Missing PO Invoice arrives with an invalid or depleted PO number. Sent back to the requester.

Industry Pain Points

  • Manual Effort: OCR (Optical Character Recognition) misreading zero as "O", causing PO lookups to fail.
  • Siloed Systems: Warehouse uses one WMS, Purchasing uses Coupa, Accounting uses NetSuite. Sync errors cause timing differences.
  • GR/IR Aging (Ghost Accruals): Goods are received, but vendor never invoices; or invoice is posted directly to expense, leaving a perpetual credit balance in GR/IR. Requires intense manual period-end reconciliation.
  • Fraud Risk: Without strict 3-way matching, bad actors can submit duplicate invoices or create dummy vendors to siphon cash.

6. Automation & Modern Solutions

The goal of modern P2P is Straight-Through Processing (STP)—invoices that touch zero human hands from receipt to payment.

  • Evaluated Receipt Settlement (ERS): The ultimate automation. The buyer completely eliminates invoices. The system automatically creates a payment voucher the moment the GRN is posted, using the GRN quantity and the PO price. Used with highly trusted vendors.
  • E-Invoicing via EDI/cXML: Bypassing PDFs entirely by transmitting structured data between vendor and buyer ERPs.
  • Key AP Automation Platforms: Coupa, Ariba (SAP), Tipalti, Bill.com, Basware. They sit in front of the ERP, process the OCR, apply ML to map lines, route exceptions automatically, and sync clean data down to the ledger.
